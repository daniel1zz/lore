## Method
- Population: the v2 population (887 ACs on live specs outside the 10 v1 D3 specs and the slice-1 spec) minus the 70 v2 sample ACs: 817 ACs.
- Relevance filter (case-insensitive regex on the statement, covering the specification and execution branches): `(tasks?|claim|evidence|verify|attest|done|archiv|plans?|phase|ac[_ -]scope|criteri|ACs?|specs?|falsif|anchor|release|ready)` -> 431 ACs.
- Draw: sorted by (spec, id); Python random.Random(20261009).sample(rows, 80); re-sorted, numbered n=1..80. 80 ACs on 45 specs. sha256 of the JSONL block: f78a20a4897dc688b32cd0a4c5185242df1f7b6b5ddd99cdb09dc2b375253593.
