Frozen AC sample v2 for the baseline pilot v2 (plan-baseline-pilot-v2-migration-method-v2-on-the-specification, task V0b). Frozen before the placement principle is written and before any mapper runs. Do not edit after creation.

## Method
- Population: every structured AC on live (approved|active) specs, excluding the 10 v1 D3 specs and the slice-1 spec: 887 ACs on 118 specs (sha256 population c5a4a8b2…b190).
- Relevance filter (fixed before sampling, case-insensitive regex on the statement): `(ACs?|acceptance|criteri|specs?|verify|evidence|falsifier|rubric|oracle|attest|gate|promot|approv)` -> 443 ACs (sha256 0b62e994…e355). Purpose: keep the sample comparable to v1's specification-focused population; an unfiltered draw would be dominated by ACs every mapper trivially places outside the branch, inflating agreement.
- Draw: rows sorted by (spec, id); Python `random.Random(20261008).sample(rows, 70)`; re-sorted by (spec, id) and numbered n=1..70. sha256 of the sample JSONL: 11a91d63042165d9743ff7f738fdd0443f9d33553f88a38ceb6a03230893f2e1. Composition: 70 ACs on 47 specs; 68 script, 2 human.
