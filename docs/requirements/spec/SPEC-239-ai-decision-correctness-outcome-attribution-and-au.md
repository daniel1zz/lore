## Overview/Goal

Answer evaluation question A: were the AI's review judgments and autonomy decisions right, and does their stated confidence mean anything (OST O2, the user's first priority, benchmark gaps 10 and 11). Today 125 of 127 overrides carry no reason (#407), confidence (mean 0.77) is never compared with outcomes, and 62 of the 132 rows counted as "overridden" are not AI judgments at all: they are verdicts of the deterministic `evidence_completeness` pre-check on draft tasks (`judge_provider = deterministic-checks`, review-checks.ts:243-256), auto-recorded as overrides when the task was approved a second later (calibration-hook.ts:102-110). Issue #408 ("the task judge carries no signal") rested on those rows and was wrong. This spec labels AI-judge decisions from machine after-events, scores judges with Brier and reliability bins, keeps deterministic verdicts out of every AI figure (BR-1422), samples judgments for an in-session human audit (κ), and makes an override without a reason impossible.

Staged requirements R-34 to R-37, R-52 (R-38 withdrawn: AUC is a phase-1 experiment output only). Baseline: leaves `baseline-score-ai-decisions-against-their-outcomes` and `baseline-audit-a-sampled-judgment` (proposed rules below); modified BR-288 (approve), BR-295 and BR-296 (reject). Depends on S-A (envelope, origin).

## Scope

- Review-judgment labels, derived on read (never stored), 30-day inclusive horizon, per-verdict scoring table (B-D9, BR-1387, BR-1411). Autonomy-decision outcomes are the outcome sweep's stored stood/reversed records, read as stored; they are scored as the reversed share of settled automatic decisions per decision class, not with Brier (B-D8, BR-1409). Coverage = labelled / eligible printed with every score.
- AI judgments only (BR-1422): every figure below counts judgments with `judge_provider` other than `deterministic-checks`; deterministic pre-check verdicts are never labelled, scored, sampled or compared with approvals (an approval against one is no override and needs no note) and are reported as a separate count per document type.
- Per judge (document type × instruction version): Brier score and 5 equal-width confidence bins over labelled judgments, minimum 20 (BR-1388). Judge AUC is not shipped; phase 1 measures it once (B-D12).
- Override share per document type with count and origin split (BR-1390). No note share: every override carries a note by construction (BR-288, BR-296).
- Guard figures beside metrics built on agent-written facts: issues per landed task, links per document (BR-1389, B-D13).
- Audit (D10): judgments sampled by hash bucket (~5% per ISO week, ordered by judgment time then id, BR-1392); no queue — `lore pending --ask` adds at most one audit question to an ask that already goes to a person; only the person's answer recorded by the ask channel stores a verdict (BR-1393); unique per judgment and person. Agreement: κ after mapping verdicts to approve/reject, 95% lower bound, prevalence, PABAK (BR-1391). Completion = audited / sampled over 4 weeks; below 50% for 4 full weeks the audit stops (BR-1410).
- Override detection: an override is detected by comparing the approval or rejection with the latest judgment of the version; no caller flag declares it or skips the note. `--override-judgment` remains only the existing audited escape needed to approve against a reject, and it still requires the note.
- Mandatory reason: an approval or rejection that overrides the latest judgment is refused with exit 4 before any write unless `--calibration-note` is non-empty; the decision and its comparison are written together, a failed comparison write fails the command with exit 1 (BR-288, BR-296; `--reason` on reject is not the note, BR-295).

## Out of scope

- Changing the deterministic pre-checks or the calibration hook that records an approval after a deterministic reject as an override: out of scope; this spec only stops such rows from counting as AI decisions (BR-1422).
- Using any of these scores to change a decision (S-E gate eligibility; BR-1005 stays true).
- Few-shot prompting of judges from override reasons (OST O2-S4 second half): needs reasons to exist first.

## Design Decisions

| # | Decision | Chosen | Rejected and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-D1 | Ground truth for calibration | Machine after-events labelled on read, calibrated by the in-session human audit (D10, B-D10) | Human audit only (cannot hold with no human present); after-events only (no check that the labels themselves are right) |
| B-D3 | Horizon | 30 days, inclusive | 14 days (main-red regressions are filed later than that in this repo's history: see #339, #351); 90 days (too few labelled decisions per week) |
| B-D4 | Sampling | Deterministic hash of the judgment id per ISO week | Random sample drawn and stored by a sampler run: two concurrent sessions would draw different samples, or need a claim |
| B-D5 | Missing override reason, and a failed note write | Refused with exit 4 before any write; the decision and its comparison with the note are one write, so a failed comparison write fails the command with exit 1 and nothing is written (BR-288, BR-296) | A warning (an agent "should remember" = not enforced); approving and only warning when the note cannot be stored (the required reason would be lost silently) |
| B-D6 | Agreement measure | Cohen's κ with confusion matrix | Raw agreement (no chance correction; the existing Wilson bound stays in `lore calibration`) |
| B-D7 | Confidence signal | Brier score and five reliability bins per judge over labelled judgments (BR-1388); autonomy decisions carry a threshold score, not a probability, so they get the reversed share per class instead (BR-1409) | Shipping AUC with a 0.65 bar (withdrawn, see B-D12); correlation (not robust to the skewed 0.77-mean confidence) |
| B-D8 | Autonomy decision labels (S3 crossdoc 1) | Read the approved outcome sweep's stored stood/reversed (AC-AU-8, 7-day rule) as is; the 30-day derive-on-read labelling (BR-1387, BR-1411) is for review judgments only (BR-1409) | Relabelling autonomy decisions with the 30-day rule (two labels for one decision); aligning BR-1387 to 7 days (review regressions arrive later than 7 days) |
| B-D2 | Store labels or derive them | Review-judgment labels are derived on read; autonomy outcomes are the sweep's existing stored records, reused not duplicated. This initiative adds no labelling writer | A new labelling sweep that writes review-judgment labels (a second writer that needs exclusivity and goes stale) |
| B-D9 | What y means per verdict (S3 eng 2) | Approve: y=1 if it stood. Reject: labelled only after an observed action — overridden approval reversed → right, stood → wrong; revised version approved → right; else unlabelled. Escalate: not scored | Treating every un-overridden reject as right (construction bias: a reject is never approved, so it would always "stand") |
| B-D10 | Human audit form (user D10; S3 crossdoc 2, DA3) | No queue: an ask an agent already puts to a person (BR-1375, `lore pending --ask`) carries at most one sampled audit question; completion (audited / sampled, 4 weeks) is reported; below 50% for 4 full weeks the audit stops (BR-1410). Differs from the queued audit AC-LG-14 removed (36 queued, 0 answered in 4 months): nothing waits in a queue, the question rides on an ask that already has a person's attention, and `lore pending` still lists 0 audit items | The weekly queued audit (already disproved by AC-LG-14); no audit at all (no check that machine labels are right) |
| B-D11 | Agreement statistic (S3 eng 4, DA3) | Map the human verdict to approve/reject, Cohen's κ with 95% lower bound, prevalence and PABAK beside it; single class → insufficient | κ on correct/incorrect vs approve/reject (category mismatch); κ alone (paradox under skewed prevalence) |
| B-D12 | Judge AUC (S3 PS2, eng 3; corrected 2026-10-25) | Not a shipped metric; measured once in phase 1 (O2-S3) over AI-judge rows only, with ≥10 per class, DeLong lower bound, single class → no result | Shipping AUC per judge with n=30 (interval ±0.2 makes the 0.65 bar noise); the earlier premise that the task judge carries no signal (#408), which rested on deterministic pre-check rows |
| B-D13 | Goodhart risk (S3 DA1) | Agents write the issues and links that grade them; every metric built on agent-written facts shows issues per landed task and links per document for the same window (BR-1389) | No guard (a falling issue rate would read as quality); excluding agent-written facts (would leave no machine after-events at all) |
| B-D15 | Note share (review rev2-2) | Not reported: overrides are refused without a note, so a note share is 1.0 by construction; AC-AD-4 tests the guarantee directly | A with-note share with a 100% target and a requirement-date window (a metric that only re-checks the spec's own invariant, plus a transitional cut-off) |
| B-D17 | Override declared or detected (review rev2-2) | Detected from the latest judgment; no flag skips the note | A caller-declared override flag (omitting it would skip the note) |
| B-D18 | Deterministic pre-check verdicts (correction 2026-10-25) | Never scored, sampled, compared with approvals or counted as overrides; reported only as a separate count per document type (BR-1422) | Scoring every review_judgments row (62 of 132 "overrides" were deterministic, which produced the false #408 finding); dropping them silently (their volume would vanish from view) |
| B-D16 | Name of the audit module | `audit-sample.ts` | `audit-queue.ts` (names a queue the design forbids, B-D10) |
| B-D14 | Where the override-reason check runs (S3 crossdoc 4) | In the approval and rejection service before any write; the flag is `--calibration-note` on both commands, `--reason` alone does not count (BR-295 modified) | In the calibration hook (runs after the approval succeeded and is never a gate); accepting `--reason` as the note (two flags for one fact) |

Falsifier literals implementers must keep: `HORIZON_DAYS = 30`, `(c - y) ** 2` and `"autonomy-outcome"` in `packages/lore/src/service/metrics/ai-decision-metrics.ts`; `% 100n < 5n`, `origin !== "human"` and `AUDIT_STOP_BELOW = 0.5` in `packages/lore/src/service/metrics/audit-sample.ts`; `1 - pe` in `packages/lore/src/service/metrics/agreement.ts`; `GUARD_METRICS` in `packages/lore/src/service/metrics/guards.ts`; `requireOverrideReason(` in `packages/lore/src/service/review-service.ts`.
