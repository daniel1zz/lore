## Overview/Goal

Answer evaluation questions B (delivery efficiency), C (delivery quality), D (concurrency) and E (unattended autonomy) from facts lore records, with every measure naming the recorder that feeds it and reporting no_data (cause source_absent) for any window its recorder does not cover, never an estimate (OST O3, O4).

This spec supersedes `spec-delivery-concurrency-and-autonomy-metrics` (S-C). Plan 1's experiment O3-S1 fired its pre-registered stop-and-re-review branch (`knowledge-experiment-results-delivery-and-concurrency-measures`): 3 of 5 delivery measures (change_fail_rate, recovery_median, rework_rate) could not be computed without manual labelling, because their recorders did not exist. S-C could not be reopened: AC-DC-6 holds attested evidence (BR-1050), so the sanctioned path is a superseding spec whose criteria supersede S-C's (`lore ac supersede`).

Staged requirements R-39, R-40, R-41. Baseline: leaves `baseline-measure-delivery`, `baseline-assess-a-plan-s-outcome`, `baseline-integrate-a-branch-into-main`, `baseline-archive-a-task` (proposed rules below); reads BR-1245 and BR-1370. Depends on S-A (envelope).

## Scope

| Metric | Question | Definition | Recorder that feeds it | Recorder live? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `delivery.lead_time_median` | B | First claim to completion after landing, median; unclaimed completions excluded and counted; a deletion is not a completion (BR-1437) | claim audit rows, archive versions | yes |
| `delivery.landings_per_day` | B | Integrate merge commits on main's first-parent line per UTC day (BR-1437, BR-1451) | main's git history | yes |
| `delivery.change_fail_rate` | C | Red records over decided landings; a record covers the landings in its commit range and counts once, in the window of its range's first landing; landings_in_red_ranges and pending beside it (BR-1438) | main-red ledger: `main-checked`, `main-red`, `main-red-green` audit rows (BR-1444) | yes, from its first `main-checked` row (landed d364a7a3, task-milestone-review-records-main-red-locally) |
| `delivery.recovery_median` | C | First landing in the red range to the recorded green, median over records turned green; open count, oldest open age and check_interval_median beside it (BR-1440) | main-red ledger (BR-1444) | yes, as above |
| `delivery.rework_rate` | C | Completed tasks whose resolved-issue record names an issue filed against landed work (14-day lookback over main's history), over all completed tasks; guard figures of S-B beside it (BR-1439) | resolved-issue records, `task-resolves` audit rows written at archive (BR-1446) | **no**: no_data source_absent until the resolves recorder task lands and covers the window |
| `concurrency.parallel_runs` | D | Live runs per hour, median and max; never-ended runs live until their last attributed write; never_ended beside it (BR-1447) | runs | yes |
| `concurrency.conflict_rate`, `.lost_race_rate` | D | Outcome share over all attempts, every outcome kind in the denominator (BR-1447, BR-1449) | integrate attempt ledger, 30 days (BR-1450) | yes |
| `autonomy.decided_without_person_share`, `.person_wait_median` | E | As BR-1448 | decision record | yes |

Coverage (BR-1445): a measure is computed for a window only when its recorder covered all of it; otherwise no_data, source_absent, naming the recorder. Landings are read from main's history, never rewritten (BR-1451), so every landing-based figure can be re-read for any past window.

## Out of scope

- Trends and colours (S-E). Per-person breakdowns. Activity counts such as commits or lines.
- Keeping attempt history longer than 30 days (BR-1449).
- Back-filling resolved-issue records or main-red records for work done before their recorder existed (that would be manual labelling, the O3-S1 kill condition).
